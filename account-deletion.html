Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your EKAMRA IPTV account and associated data at any time. Your request will be processed within 30 days of submission.

Request Deletion via Email

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Click the button above or send an email to info@ekamraott.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - EKAMRA IPTV"
  3. Include your registered email address or username in the email body
  4. We will confirm receipt of your request within 48 hours
  5. Your account and associated data will be deleted within 30 days

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account credentials (username, email, password)
  • Profile information (name, profile picture, phone number)
  • Watch history and viewing preferences
  • Subscription and payment records
  • Saved playlists and favorites
  • Downloaded content associated with your account
  • App settings and preferences

Data That May Be Retained

The following data may be retained for legal, security, or operational purposes:

  • Transaction records (retained for up to 5 years for tax/legal compliance)
  • Fraud prevention logs (retained for up to 90 days)
  • Server access logs (retained for up to 90 days for security auditing)

What Happens After Deletion?

  • You will lose access to your account immediately after deletion is processed
  • All purchased subscriptions will be cancelled (no refunds for partial periods)
  • Downloaded content will no longer be accessible
  • You can create a new account at any time in the future
